La Maestria

Four distinct stages of devotion. Each piece undergoes over 80 hours of meticulous handcrafting in our Milanese workshop.

Phase 01

I. The Sculpted Vision

Every design begins with gouache watercolor sketches and hand-carved wax prototypes to establish balance and fluidity.

Phase 02

II. Lost-Wax Casting

Molten 18k solid gold is poured into custom ceramic molds, transforming delicate wax sculptures into permanent metal.

Phase 03

III. Micro-Pavé Setting

Master setters place each diamond under microscopic precision, securing each stone with delicate gold prongs.

Phase 04

IV. Mirror Polishing

Hand-polishing with soft linen threads brings out the deep warmth and silky lustre signature to Sedi gold.
