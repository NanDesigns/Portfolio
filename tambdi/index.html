01 / Story

My mother never wrote a recipe down. I wrote them all down wrong.

Tambdi means red, the colour of the laterite in Assagao where my family cooked for four generations. We opened in Bandra in 2019 with a coal oven, a borrowed grinding stone and eleven dishes.

We still cure our own sausages and ferment our own toddy vinegar. The fish comes from Sassoon Dock at five in the morning, and the pão is baked by a man in Chimbai who will not give us his name.

Anselmo D'Costa, chef
